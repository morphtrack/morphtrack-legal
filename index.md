# MorphTrack

**MorphTrack** is a private, photo-based progress tracker. It helps you monitor
visible changes in your skin, body, posture, facial symmetry, hair and other
personal goals over time — from your own photos, on your own terms.

## What you can do

- Organize photos into personal **trackers**, one per goal.
- **Compare** any two photos side by side to see how things changed.
- Log your daily **routine and factors** (skincare, sport, sleep, diet).
- Run an **AI analysis** — on demand — that compares your photos over time and
  describes what visibly changed, zone by zone, and how it lines up with your
  routine.

## Privacy first

Your photos are visible only to you and stored securely in the EU (Frankfurt).
All camera metadata, including location, is removed from every photo at upload.
There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking; we never sell your data or
use it to train AI models. You can delete any photo, tracker, or your whole
account at any time.

## Sign in with Google and Apple

MorphTrack offers **Sign in with Google** and **Sign in with Apple** purely to
authenticate you and create or access your account. We request only your basic
profile information — your email address and, if provided, your name. We do not
access your contacts, files, or any other Google or Apple data, and we do not
post anything on your behalf.

# Morph — Support

_Updated: 19 October 2026_

Читать по-русски: [Поддержка](https://www.morphtrack.app/v2/ru/support)

**Morph (MorphTrack)** is a photo-based skin and body change tracker with on-demand AI observations.

## Contact us

For questions, issues, or feedback, email **[morphtrack.app@gmail.com](mailto:morphtrack.app@gmail.com)**.

For a technical issue, include:

- What you were doing and what happened, including any error message.
- Your device model, iOS or Android version, and app version.
- A screenshot if helpful, with private photos and personal details hidden.

Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, payment card details, medical records or unnecessary skin/body photos.

## Common questions

**How do I run an AI analysis?** Add at least two photos to a tracker, then tap "Run AI analysis" on the tracker screen.

**How do I manage or cancel my subscription?** Use the store account you made the purchase with:

- **iPhone:** Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MorphTrack. [Apple instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428).
- **Android:** Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → MorphTrack. [Google Play instructions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481?hl=en).

Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancellation is handled separately; it is not a prerequisite for requesting account deletion.

**How do I delete my account?** In the app: More → Profile → Delete account. Details: [account deletion](https://www.morphtrack.app/v2/delete-account).

## Requests about your data

Email **[morphtrack.app@gmail.com](mailto:morphtrack.app@gmail.com)** to request access to or a copy of your data, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent. Use the subject **Privacy request** and describe what you want us to do. If possible, write from your account email; we may need to verify account ownership.

**Cannot access the app?** You can request deletion by email with the subject **Delete my account**. Include your account email and whether you want the whole account or specific data deleted. Tell us if you have lost email access or used Apple's Hide My Email. You do not need to reinstall the app, create a new account or pay. See the [deletion procedure](https://www.morphtrack.app/v2/delete-account).

**To stop future AI sharing**, open More → About → AI and your data, or contact us. Withdrawing consent does not delete previous analyses or copies already sent; request deletion separately if needed.

Request handling, applicable deadlines and appeals are explained in the [Health Data Policy](https://www.morphtrack.app/v2/consumer-health-data) and [Privacy Policy](https://www.morphtrack.app/v2/privacy-policy).

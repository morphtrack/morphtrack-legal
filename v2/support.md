## Common questions

**How do I run an AI analysis?** Add at least two photos to a tracker, then tap "Run AI analysis" on the tracker screen.

**How do I manage or cancel my subscription?** Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought them in: App Store — Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions; Google Play — Play Store → Profile → Payments and subscriptions. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

**How do I delete my account?** In the app: More → Profile → Delete account. Details: [account deletion](delete-account).

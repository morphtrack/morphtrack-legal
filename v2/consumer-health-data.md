## What data, from which sources, and why

We collect from you photos of skin/body areas, photo notes and dates, tracker names/goals, routine and ongoing factors, and optional profile details such as skin type, activity, sleep and smoking. These may describe physical conditions, symptoms, care or wellbeing. We also receive any health information you choose to include in a support request.

AI observations are derived from the photos and context submitted for an analysis. These inferences, and account identifiers linked to the journal, may also be consumer health data. We do not obtain your medical records from healthcare providers or buy health profiles from data brokers.

We use this information to store and display your private journal, compare selected photos, generate requested AI observations and answer your support requests. Necessary operational/security processing protects these features. We do not use it to determine eligibility for employment, insurance or credit.

Internal dashboards use account identifiers, recent authenticated activity, and the dates, counts, types and statuses of registrations, photos, trackers, factors, routine marks, analyses and subscriptions to evaluate active/returning use, feature adoption and subscription conversion. Reports may also group tracker and factor names, including names you enter. Those names can reveal health information. Calculations may include journal records saved before you opted in. Photo contents, notes, analysis text, optional profile details, email addresses and device details are not used in these product-statistics queries. Data is processed by the infrastructure providers described below; it is not sent to OpenAI for statistics.

The source records remain account-linked even when a report displays totals. Refusing statistics does not restrict the journal, AI or purchases. After an account moves to the updated permission protocol, it is excluded from product dashboards until you give separate, explicit statistics permission. Before that transition, accounts used only in older versions retain the previous server-side statistics without a new consent prompt, unless you have already declined statistics or withdrawn journal permission. This compatibility mode does not create a consent record. Updating the app while staying signed in also ends the previous mode until you make your choice. A refusal or withdrawal applies on all devices: returning to an older version does not turn statistics back on. In versions with this control, turn off statistics in **More → About → AI and your data**, or contact us. Withdrawal excludes your account from future calculations and clears the recent-activity timestamp; it does not automatically delete the underlying journal or subscription records needed for their original purposes. Withdrawing journal permission also stops AI and statistics. Necessary sign-in, payment, error and security monitoring remains separate and does not depend on this optional choice. Website analytics is separate too.

## Who receives it and for what purpose

- **DigitalOcean:** backend, account database and private photo storage in Frankfurt, Germany. From 9 November 2026, new database backups are also created, checked in an isolated restore environment, encrypted and stored here with 30-day retention. Database copies can contain notes, factors and saved AI results; original photo files are stored separately and are not included. Private encrypted hourly deletion-recovery exports are retained for 90 days and contain limited identifiers, object types and deletion/withdrawal times, not journal text or photo files. The live recovery journal currently has no automatic expiry; its purpose and retention review are described in the Privacy Policy.
- **Cloudflare** delivers and protects the API, processing requests and their contents, including journal data sent through the API. Processing may occur outside Germany.
- **GitHub Actions:** source code, CI and job orchestration with technical completion statuses. New backup and restore-check jobs run in DigitalOcean and do not send plaintext journal records or backup files to GitHub. Previously created private encrypted GitHub artifacts remain until their original expiration: database copies for up to 30 days and deletion-recovery exports for up to 90 days after creation. Access is restricted to recovery work; GitHub processing may occur outside Germany.
- **OpenAI** receives selected photos, dates, tracker/factor context and opted-in notes to generate an analysis you request.
- **Google/Gmail** processes information you include in support correspondence.
- **Authorised service personnel** have restricted access for support, operation, security, the product statistics described above or legal compliance. Limited disclosure may also be required by law or for legal claims.

We do not share consumer health data with affiliated companies. Payment/sign-in providers and Resend receive information necessary for those functions, not journal content through those functions. Website hosting/analytics is described in the general Privacy Policy.

We do not sell consumer health data or use it for advertising or model training. We do not authorise third parties to collect journal data through our service to track a person's health-related activity over time across unaffiliated websites/apps. We do not use precise location or geofences to identify visits to healthcare facilities, or face recognition to identify people.

## Your consent and controls

Collection or sharing must have the consent or other specific basis required by applicable law. An exception for processing necessary to provide a requested service, where available, does not authorise unrelated purposes or replace explicit consent required elsewhere. **This policy and acceptance of Terms are not a substitute for a separate required consent.** In app versions that support the updated choices, one screen explains journal processing, optional OpenAI sharing and optional product statistics. You separately confirm that you are 18 or older and accept the Terms/acknowledge the Privacy Policy, then choose each processing permission independently. The processing choices start off; continuing records only the choices you actually make, with the disclosure version and date. AI and statistics are optional. Older app versions may not show all controls. Their transitional statistics mode is described in the product-statistics section; using an older version or accepting Terms is not treated as separate consent. Update the app or contact us to manage these choices. Once saved, account permissions apply across devices, including older versions.

AI sharing is optional. Review or withdraw journal permission and future AI sharing in **More → About → AI and your data**. Withdrawing journal permission also stops new AI requests and product statistics. New journal entries and changes that require this permission are blocked; reading and deleting existing content remain available. Withdrawal does not cancel a store subscription. You can also contact us to withdraw consent or stop particular processing. Withdrawal does not undo earlier lawful processing or erase previously saved results; you can request their deletion too.

## Access, correction and deletion

Email **morphtrack.app@gmail.com**, subject **Privacy request**, to request confirmation, access, correction, withdrawal or deletion. Where applicable, you may request a list of health-data recipients, including affiliates/third parties, and their contact details. Tell us the action you want; no new account or payment is required.

We verify identity proportionately through existing account access or verified control of its email, without requesting your password or unnecessary health details. An authorised representative may submit a request, subject to verification of authority. We do not discriminate for exercising these rights.

You can edit your profile/notes and delete content in the app. Report an inaccurate AI observation or delete it; we cannot guarantee that every generated statement can be edited in place. To remove the whole account, use **More → Profile → Delete account**. Deleting a photo alone does not remove associated AI results. See the [deletion procedure](https://www.morphtrack.app/v2/delete-account).

## Response times and retained copies

We handle requests without undue delay. For applicable Washington health-data requests, the ordinary response period is 45 days from receipt. Under Nevada's health-data rules, ordinary responses are due within 45 days after authentication, but deletion and notification to recipients are due within 30 days after authentication. A permitted extension is explained before the initial response period ends. Earlier applicable deadlines, including GDPR's one-month response period, still apply.

We notify recipients of deletion where required. Any legally permitted delay for restricted backup copies is separate from active-data deletion; it is not permission to continue ordinary use. Under Washington's rules this backup delay cannot exceed six months after authentication. We explain applicable exceptions rather than promising that every provider copy disappears instantly. The general Privacy Policy describes retention.

## Appeal and complaints

If we refuse your request, you may reply with subject **Privacy appeal**. We review it and explain the outcome within 45 days of receipt, or an earlier applicable deadline.

You may also complain to a competent authority. This is a right, not a required step, and our internal appeal does not limit your statutory complaint rights. The appropriate authority depends on the law that applies:

- **EEA/UK data-protection law:** the competent data-protection supervisory authority.
- **Serbian data-protection law:** the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection.
- **US state law:** where your case is covered by a particular state's law, that state's Attorney General, for example [Washington](https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint), [Nevada](https://ag.nv.gov/Complaints/File_Complaint/) or [Connecticut](https://portal.ct.gov/ag). These are not universal complaint destinations for all users.
- **Other jurisdictions:** the authority competent under the applicable local law.

Residence is not always the only test of coverage. For example, Washington's health-data law can also cover a person whose consumer health data is collected in Washington, even if they do not live there.

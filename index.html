Good to know

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Write us at morphtrack.app@gmail.com — a person replies.

Is it actually private?

Yes. Your photos are visible only to you and stored securely in the EU (Frankfurt). All camera metadata, including location, is removed at upload. No ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking — we never sell your data or use it to train AI models, and you can delete any photo, tracker, or your whole account at any time.

What can I track?

Anything visible you want to follow over time — skin and acne, body and posture, facial symmetry, hair, or a personal goal of your own. Each gets its own tracker and timeline.

How does the AI reading work?

It runs only when you ask. MorphTrack compares the photos in a tracker across time and describes, zone by zone, what visibly changed — then lines it up with the routine you logged. It's a plain-language observation, not medical advice or a diagnosis.

Is it free?

MorphTrack is free to start. A free account can run a limited number of AI readings each month; MorphTrack Pro unlocks unlimited analyses.

How do I sign in?

With email, or one tap using Google or Apple. Sign-in is only for syncing your own account across devices.

Straight answers

Before you download.

What do I actually get?

A private timeline of your own photos, grouped by tracker and by month. A compare view that puts any two dates against each other. A calendar where you tick off the routine you actually followed. And an AI reading, run when you ask for it, that describes what visibly changed and which of your logged factors line up with it.

Is it free?

Photos, comparisons, the calendar and an unlimited number of routine factors are free. A free account can run a limited number of AI readings each month; MorphTrack Pro unlocks unlimited analyses.

How many habits can I track?

There is no cap. Add as many factors as the treatment needs, across as many trackers as you like — no slot limits, no per-tracker fees.

How long before it tells me anything?

Two comparable photos are enough for a first reading. A trend takes three to four weeks — that is roughly how long a real change needs to separate itself from a difference in light or angle.

Is this medical advice?

No. MorphTrack describes what is visible in your photos and notes which logged factors line up with it. Those are correlations worth checking, not causes, and not a diagnosis. For anything that worries you, see a doctor.
